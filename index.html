Build Your 4-Week Program

Set your starting weights and increments, pick your accessory work, and generate a printable 4-week log — sourced from the book's own rep schemes and reset math, not generic defaults.

Core mechanic — 2x5, 1x5+: squat, bench, press and front squat are worked for two sets of five, then a third set taken to failure (not stopped at 5). Deadlift is one hard working set only.
Standard increments: squat/deadlift +5 lb (2 kg) per session · press/bench +2.5 lb (1 kg) per session.
Doubling rule: if your last set goes past 10 reps, consider doubling next session's jump instead of holding pace.
The reset: when you fail to hit 5 reps on the last set, drop to 90% of that weight (round down to the nearest increment) and climb back up at your normal pace — you'll usually set new rep records on the way back up before you retake the old sticking point.

2DAILY WORK — THE BODYWEIGHT LAYER
The book's "Section Two: Bodyweight Training." Runs every day, independent of which lifting day it is — never taken to failure, always a little more than last week.

METHOD

Multiple easy sets spread throughout the day, never to failure, one full day off per week. Add a set or a rep once the last rep of every set still feels easy.

3CONDITIONING — CARDIO
Sections Three & Four of the book: High Intensity Conditioning and Low Intensity Conditioning. Optional layers on top of the first two.

High Intensity Conditioning

COUNT
The book's "10-minute rule": keep these short and brutally intense — 10 minutes is about the ceiling. Default placement: right after a lifting day, plus one standalone session (e.g. the weekend).

Low Intensity Conditioning

COUNT
MINUTES
The book's preferred low-intensity tool: fasted walking, done often. Its value comes from frequency, not intensity — it's the least "sexy" layer but effective for fat loss.

The 20-Minute Aerobic Solution

A 20-minute RPE-ladder interval session on any cardio machine — effort climbs 5→9 in waves, capped with an all-out minute 19.

Villain Challenge 1

STAGE
Train six days per week. Start with 3 sets of 10 in 30 seconds; add 1-2 reps only after making the target time. Add three seconds for each added rep and reduce rest toward one minute. At 3 x 30 in 90 seconds, move to 2 sets; at 2 x 50 in 2:30, move to a single 100-rep set.
